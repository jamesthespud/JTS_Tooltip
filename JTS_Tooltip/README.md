# JTS Tooltip

Part of the **JTS Suite** of addons for World of Warcraft: Forever, by James.

A clean, simple replacement for the default unit tooltip.

## What it shows
- **Players:** full name (first and last), level, guild, guild rank, race, class, faction and who they are targeting. Name and class are in class colour, guild in faction colour, level in difficulty colour.
- **NPCs:** name, title, type, level (with Elite, Rare or Boss), reaction, faction and target.
- **Combat pets:** name, owner, type, level, faction and target.
- **Companion pets:** name, owner, type and faction.

## Options
Esc > Options > AddOns > JTS Tooltip, or type `/jtstt`.
- Anchor: follow the mouse cursor, drag anywhere (Custom), or one of nine screen positions
- Horizontal and vertical offset, scale, lock position
- Hide Blizzard's tooltip, show on mouseover, show your target when not hovering, hide when nothing is selected
- Turn players, NPCs, and pets & companions on or off

## Slash commands
- `/jtstt` - open options
- `/jtstt toggle` - turn on or off
- `/jtstt preview` - show the tooltip for 4 seconds
- `/jtstt anchor <cursor|custom|topleft|top|topright|left|center|right|bottomleft|bottom|bottomright>`
- `/jtstt lock` - lock or unlock dragging
- `/jtstt reset` - reset position
- `/jtstt blizzard` - hide or show Blizzard's tooltip

## Install
Unzip into `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns` so you have an `AddOns\JTS_Tooltip` folder, then restart the game.

## Reporting bugs
Copy the full error text (or take a screenshot), note what you were hovering or targeting, and post it on the addon's GitHub or CurseForge page.
